World on Rotation pins mixes and playlists to the places their music comes from. A capital city, a port town, a mountain village, a whole region or a whole country: spin the globe, pick a place and press play on a selection put together by someone who knows its sound.

Every mix and playlist is chosen by a person. Each selector has a page of their own, with a short bio, their links and everything they have pinned to the map. Everything plays through Mixcloud, which is licensed, so the artists and labels behind the music get paid.

The music comes with context. Every country has a guide to its sound: the traditions and instruments, the eras that shaped it, the music its diaspora carried abroad, what is happening now, and the labels, festivals and records worth tracking down. Places get guides of their own, down to where to hear live music, where to buy records and what to tune in to.

If you would rather not choose, the turntables will choose for you: one record for each half of the world, starting with a random mix from your side of it. Every place you play adds a stamp to your own passport, kept in your browser.

Who the selectors are

A selector is anyone who knows the music of a place from the inside: DJs and record collectors, radio hosts, musicians, composers and sound artists, label owners, music journalists, archivists, ethnomusicologists and field recordists, and the people who run the nights, collectives and community spaces where a scene happens.

We give priority to selectors from the Global South and from diaspora communities, to women, trans, non-binary and queer voices, and to people rooted in their scene, whatever the size of their following.

Who is behind it

World on Rotation is a project of Rhythm Passport, the independent world music platform based in London, with its online magazine, London gig guide and live events.
