Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. World on Rotation sets none for ordinary visitors: no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only cookies you may meet come from the music players and from the editors’ login.

Cookies from the music players

Mixes play through Mixcloud and some playlists through Spotify. When their players load on a page, those companies may set their own cookies, for example to remember your settings, count plays or keep you signed in to your account with them. We do not control these cookies and cannot read them. For details, see Mixcloud’s privacy policy and Spotify’s cookie policy.

Cookies for editors

The people who run the site log in through WordPress. Their browsers get the standard WordPress login cookies (with names starting wordpress_ and wp-settings-) and a security cookie from Wordfence (starting wfwaf-authcookie). These are needed to log in and keep the dashboard secure, and visitors who never log in do not get them.

Your passport is not a cookie

The stamps in your passport are saved in your browser’s local storage, under the name rp-atlas-passport. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies, including the players’ cookies, in your browser settings. If you block them, the site still works, though the Mixcloud and Spotify players may not. Deleting this site’s data in your browser also clears your passport.
