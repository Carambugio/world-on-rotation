This page explains what personal information is handled when you visit World on Rotation, who handles it and what you can do about it. The short version: there are no public accounts, no analytics and no adverts, so we collect very little from visitors. Selectors we invite to send mixes tell us a little more about themselves, and this page explains what happens to that too.

Who we are

World on Rotation (worldonrotation.com) is run by [wor_legal_name], the company behind Rhythm Passport, based in London. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can contact us at [wor_contact].

What we collect

Server and security logs. Like every website, ours runs on a server that records basic technical information about each visit: your IP address, browser type, the pages you asked for and when. Our host, Hostinger, keeps these logs to run the site and keep it secure. Our security plugin, Wordfence, also checks visitors’ IP addresses and requests to block attacks and spam. We use this information only for security and to fix problems.

Nothing else from visitors. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and visitors cannot sign up or comment on the site. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and keep them only as long as the conversation needs.

If you send us a mix

Submissions are by invitation only, through a personal link we send you. The form asks for your name or alias, and optionally your full name; your email; your nationality and where you are based; what you do; a short bio; your links; a photo or logo; and the title, audio file, tracklist, place, styles and description of your mix.

We use these details to review your submission and, if we publish it, to show your profile and your mix on the site. We upload the mix and its tracklist to the World on Rotation account on Mixcloud, credited to you, where Mixcloud’s privacy policy also applies. We delete our copy of the audio file once it is on Mixcloud. Your email is never shown: we use it only to tell you about your submission and to contact you about World on Rotation. To stop the form being abused, we also keep a scrambled version of your connection’s IP address for one hour.

We rely on your consent, which you give when you tick the box on the form. You can ask us at any time to change or remove your profile, your mix or your details, and we will. If we decide not to publish a submission, we delete it.

What stays in your browser

Your passport. Each place you play adds a stamp to your passport. The stamps are saved in your own browser’s local storage and never sent to us. Clearing your browser’s data for this site removes them.

Your side of the world. The turntables start with a mix from your half of the globe. To decide which half, the page reads the time zone set on your device. This happens in your browser, and the time zone is not sent to us or stored.

Services that load content on our pages

Some parts of the site come from other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies receive your IP address and basic browser information, and some may set their own cookies. Each one is responsible for what it does with that information under its own privacy policy.

  • Mixcloud plays the mixes. Its player loads when you open a mix or the turntables page, and Mixcloud may set cookies. See Mixcloud’s privacy policy.
  • Spotify plays some of the playlists, through its embedded player. See Spotify’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) and jsDelivr deliver the code libraries that draw the globe.

Why we can use this information

Under UK data protection law, we rely on our legitimate interests in running a secure, working website, and on your consent for what you send us through the submission form. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use it to profile you.

Your rights

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how we use it. Write to us at [wor_contact] and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
